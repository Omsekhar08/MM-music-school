Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to own an instrument before attending the demo class?
No! We provide studio instruments for all practice and trial sessions. You can try out different instruments at the academy before deciding which one you want to purchase.
Is there an age limit to join classes?
Music is for everyone! We teach young children (from 5+ years old), students, working professionals, and retired music lovers. The curriculum is customized based on your age and learning speed.
Do you provide recognized certification?
Yes. We prepare students for graded examinations and recognized certifications, enabling you to build official musical qualifications for stage performance and professional portfolios.
Can I attend classes online if I cannot visit Korlagunta?
Yes! We offer live, interactive 1-on-1 online music classes with HD video, high-fidelity audio feedback, and shared digital notes.
